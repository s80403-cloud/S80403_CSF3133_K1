Overview and History

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. Situated right along the coast of the South China Sea, UMT is Malaysia's premier institution focused on marine science, aquatic resources, maritime technology, and coastal sustainability.

The university started as the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in Mengabang Telipot in 1979. In May 1999, the Cabinet approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) as an associate campus of UPM, before gaining autonomy in 2001 as KUSTEM. On 1 February 2007, it was officially upgraded to full university status and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across marine sciences, environmental studies, computer science, maritime informatics, engineering, and business management. Its strategic coastal location allows students and researchers direct access to marine ecosystems, including nearby islands such as Bidong Island, where the university maintains a dedicated marine research station.
